Life while working for a game studio, owned by Microsoft, in 2025.

Layoff culture has become the norm. Last week, July 2nd 2025, Microsoft announced another round of layoffs in their XBOX org where they cut 9000 positions.

...

A similar layoff happened in July 2026. This time new XBOX CEO announced 1600 people would be let go effective immediately (July 6th) and another 1600 for a total of 3200 laid off for FY27. This round affected way more people that I worked with every day for years. Those of us that remain, in the empty shell of an office or remote, feel like we're just riding the ship down.
